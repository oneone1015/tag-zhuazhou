What is Zhuazhou? (抓周)

In Taiwanese and Chinese tradition, a baby's first birthday is celebrated with a special ritual called Zhuazhou (pronounced "jwah-joe"). Objects are spread out in front of the baby, and whatever she reaches for first is said to reveal her destiny — her talents, career, or character.

How does it work?

Items representing different life paths are laid out on a mat or table. The baby crawls or toddles toward the display, completely unguided, and grabs whatever catches her eye. No steering allowed — it's all up to her!

Some families believe it's prophetic. Others just love watching a baby make her grand declaration. Either way, it's delightful.

🚪 The Clever Gate (過聰明門)

Before the Zhuazhou grab, many Taiwanese families perform a ritual called Guò Cōngmíng Mén — literally "passing through the clever gate." The baby is carried through an arch or doorway decorated with symbolic items, believed to bless her with intelligence, good health, and a bright future.

Each item is held up, a blessing is spoken, and everyone joins in together.

🚪 The Gate

Loved one: "We pass Tag through the Clever Gate — a threshold of love, between who she has been and everything she is yet to become."

With one voice, we say: "Welcome, Tag. The world is yours."

🧅 Green Onion — 蔥 (Cōng)

Loved one: "Cōng sounds like 'clever' in Mandarin."

With one voice, we say: "May Tag's mind be a bright and beautiful thing."

🧄 Garlic — 蒜 (Suàn)

Loved one: "Garlic is for the ones who see clearly — who move through the world with intention and grace."

With one voice, we say: "May Tag always find her way."

🥜 Peanut — 花生 (Huāshēng)

Loved one: "Peanut means 'blooming life' — roots that go deep, a life that grows full."

With one voice, we say: "May Tag bloom in every season."

🍊 Persimmon — 柿子 (Shìzi)

Loved one: "Shì sounds like 'everything' — everything as you wish it to be."

With one voice, we say: "May all things unfold beautifully for Tag."

🍎 Apple — 蘋果 (Píngguǒ)

With one voice, we say: "May Tag always come home to peace."

🌿 Celery — 芹菜 (Qíncài)

Loved one: "Qín sounds like 'diligent' — the quiet dedication that makes great things possible."

With one voice, we say: "May Tag love the work of her hands."

🍗 Chicken Leg — 雞腿 (Jītuǐ)

Loved one: "The chicken leg is a wish that she will never want for anything — always held, always fed, always enough."

With one voice, we say: "May Tag always have more than enough."

💰 Gold Ingot — 元寶 (Yuánbǎo)

Loved one: "The gold ingot carries a wish for abundance — not just in wealth, but in joy, in love, in a life well lived."

With one voice, we say: "May Tag's life be rich in every way that matters."
